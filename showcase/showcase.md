# Find your angle.

Carom is a paddle duel with a twist in every return. A luminous ball, two paddles, and two gold obstacles turn a familiar court into a game of timing and unexpected angles. Take on the machine in Solo, or settle things with a friend in local Versus.

The opening serve gives you a moment to line up. Catch the ball in the middle of your paddle for a direct return, or meet it near the edge to send it wide. Every paddle hit raises the pace, so a comfortable rally can quickly become a scramble.

Move as you make contact and the ball carries your spin across the court. Curve a return around an obstacle, bank it off a wall, or let the gold bars send your opponent the long way around. The short glowing trail lets you read the bend as it happens.

First to eleven wins, but you need a two-point lead. At ten-all, each serve is another chance to change the angle. Stay calm, keep moving, and make the next shot yours.
